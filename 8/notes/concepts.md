
### **2. Required Concepts (TL;DR)**

#### Key Concepts:
- **Express.js**: A minimal Node.js framework for building web applications.
- **Middleware**: Functions executed during the request-response cycle.
- **Routing in Express**: Mapping URLs to specific handlers using `app.get`, `app.post`, and route modules.
